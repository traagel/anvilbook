What this is

anvilbook is a small program you run on your own computer. It reads the prices that the Auctionator addon saves in WoW Forever, keeps every scan as history, and works out what is worth crafting with the recipes your characters know.

Where these prices come from

Players who run anvilbook and turn sharing on send their scans here. A realm is only as fresh as its last contributor, and nothing is shared unless the player asks for it.
